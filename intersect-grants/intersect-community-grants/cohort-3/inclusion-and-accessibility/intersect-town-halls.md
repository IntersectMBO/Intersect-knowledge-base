# Intersect Town Halls

## Swarm

Swarm Team will lead both monthly Intersect Town Halls and After Town Halls, providing a connected experience for attendees as well as providing important elements (publicity, planning, community research) in one. They will ensure that Town Halls and After Town Halls are engaging, welcoming, well-publicised, and participatory.&#x20;
