# Stable Coin

Grantee will conduct preliminary research into the feasibility, advantages, and challenges of onboarding USDT (Tether) and USDC (USD Coin) natively onto the Cardano blockchain. This research aims to explore potential benefits, risks, technical considerations and costs associated with integrating these stablecoins into the Cardano ecosystem.&#x20;

**Grant value: 66,000 ADA**
