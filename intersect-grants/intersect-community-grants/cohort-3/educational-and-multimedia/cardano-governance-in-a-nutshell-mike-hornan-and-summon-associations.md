# Cardano Governance in a Nutshell - Mike Hornan & Summon Associations

Mike Hornan and Adam Rusch will enable continuation of Mike Hornan’s “Cardano Governance in a Nutshell” series of infographics and provide a structure of support to develop video tutorials and live workshops through the beginning of the Voltaire era. These infographics will provide an easy and accessible way for ADA holders to understand how Cardano Governance works. The governance workshop series will run for 20 weeks and provide ADA holders with mentorship and opportunities to explore governance scenarios.

**Grant value: 100,000 ADA**
