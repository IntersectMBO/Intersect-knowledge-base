# Making Cardano governance clear and accessible to everyone, Portuguese speaking community

Daniela Alves and Maria Carmo will establish engaging tutorials, detailed educational materials, and monthly online meetings to make Cardano governance clear and accessible to everyone, with a focus on the Portuguese speaking community.&#x20;

**Grant value: 45,000 ADA**
