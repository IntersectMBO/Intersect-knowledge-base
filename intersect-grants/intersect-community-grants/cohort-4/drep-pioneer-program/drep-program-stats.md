# DRep Program Stats

The **DRep Pioneer Program** was an outstanding success. Completed in September 2024, the program saw exceptional dedication and effort from the **15 DRep leaders**, who conducted **44 workshops** across **12 countries** in **7 different languages**. This initiative reached over **400 participants**, equipping them with the knowledge and skills needed to actively engage and contribute in the new governance role of DRep.&#x20;
