# Consensus 2024 Cardano Marketing

## Rare Network, Texas, USA - 29th May 2024

**Grant value: 75,000 ADA**

Rare Network is one of the chosen grantee that will organize a Consensus 2024 Cardano Side Event with Media & Marketing for Intersect MBO and its partners (EMURGO, Genius Yield, Genius X) for around 200-300 people in a venue located in downtown Austin, TX. Grantee will provide film crew and media coverage from this side event as well as from the Consensus 2024 conference. Grantee will also promote a side event on their social media to assure meeting attendance numbers.
