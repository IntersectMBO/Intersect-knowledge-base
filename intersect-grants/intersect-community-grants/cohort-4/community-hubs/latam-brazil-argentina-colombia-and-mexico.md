# LATAM (Brazil, Argentina, Colombia & Mexico)

**Month 1**

* Setup of hub and bootstrap (Communication of hub launch, definition of regional town halls, date setting and invites to LATAM community).
* Translation of initial communications content (value proposition - Spanish / Portuguese).

**Month 2**

* First monthly LATAM Town Hall meeting (detail capture).
* Opening of paid membership.
* Community Hub Page live on Intersect site.

**Month 3**

* Quarterly meeting with Intersect leadership to discuss results, and present findings, plans and needs/support for the next quarter.
* Second monthly LATAM Town Hall meeting (detail capture).
* Start to attract and sign up LATAM companies.
* Communicate membership benefits.

**Month 4**

* Brazil Hub Manager appointed.
* Launch Portuguese Town Hall (detail capture).
* LATAM Town Hall meeting Spanish and Portuguese (detail capture).
* Generate driven community insight and feedback.

**Month 5**

* Argentina hub launched and Hub Manager appointed.
* LATAM Town Hall meeting Spanish and Portuguese (detail capture).

**Month 6**

* Colombia hub launched and Hub Manager appointed.
* Quarterly meeting with Intersect leadership to discuss results, and present findings, plans and needs/support for the next quarter.
* LATAM Town Hall meeting Spanish and Portuguese (detail capture).

**Month 7**

* Mexico hub launched and Hub Manager appointed.
* LATAM Town Hall meeting Spanish and Portuguese (detail capture).
* 2025 hub proposal including costed activities. 2025 membership fee setting.
* Present LATAM hub at AMM.
* Present LATAM hub at Constitutional Convention.
