# Local face-to-face events and meetups

During the summer of 2024, Intersect awarded grants to support local, in-person meet-ups focused on Intersect and Cardano governance. In the following pages, you will find a blog, complete with photos from each of the 14 events held around the world, including in Australia, Portugal, the USA, Estonia, Venezuela, the DRC, Nigeria, Paraguay, Vietnam, and Brazil.

These meet-ups created valuable opportunities for individuals to connect, network, and gain insights into Cardano governance, contributing to the growth of both Intersect and the broader Global Cardano Ecosystem.
