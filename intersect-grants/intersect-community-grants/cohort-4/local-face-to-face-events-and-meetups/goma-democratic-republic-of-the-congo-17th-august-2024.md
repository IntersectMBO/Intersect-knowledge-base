# Goma, Democratic Republic of the Congo - 17th August 2024

## [Community meet-up Goma blog](https://mwatsimulamo.blogspot.com/2024/08/intersect-meetup-in-town-of-goma.html)

On August 31st 2024, the city of Goma vibrated to the rhythm of the Cardano blockchain. Organized by Olivier MWATSIMULAMO, the local event attracted 38 enthusiastic participants. The event featured cutting-edge presentations: Boaz Bandu delved into the fundamentals of blockchain and Cardano, Paul MUPENDA provided an in-depth look at Cardano's governance, and Martin Musagara explored the intricacies of Intersect.
