# Curitiba, Brazil - 4th September 2024

## [Community Meet Up in Brazil blog  ](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1iz-msA5M7iWT8Wt-5oY90_9uSVFr-e4g/edit?usp=sharing\&ouid=104648571819070702900\&rtpof=true\&sd=true)

On September 4th 2024, a Community Meet-Up was held at Hard Rock Café in Curitiba, decentralized governance and its opportunities was explored within the Cardano ecosystem. The event, organized by Daniela Alves and Maria Carmo from GovChainLab with operational support from Token Economy of Curitiba, was a success, with all 30 spots filled. The workshop gathered a diverse group of participants, including members of the Cardano community, representatives from other blockchains, players in the crypto market, and the HIPE retail innovation hub of Curitiba. We were also honored to have a representative from the Federal Technological University of Paraná, who requested this same workshop to be held at their institution, highlighting the impact generated by the event.

_Daniela Alves "We are very happy and proud to see that we were able to deliver excellence, with high quality and overwhelmingly positive feedback. Over time, this has generated strong and important partnerships in Brazil, which also certifies that all the work has been conducted seriously, always with the concern of delivering value, knowledge, and a unique experience for the community and other participants."_
