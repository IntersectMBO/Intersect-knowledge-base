# Rio de Janeiro, Brazil - July 24th to 26th

## [Community meet-up Rio de Janeiro Blog](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NReEAfDx4DmJmrZkcdUmOM1FfJKt_ldyTLern9UfqZA/edit)

The Blockchain Rio 2024 event, held from July 24th to 26th, was a significant milestone for the blockchain industry in Brazil. During the event, Thiago Nunes was responsible for attracting new members and presenting the advantages of being part of Intersect. In addition to recruiting new members, Thiago served as a point of contact to facilitate the connection between companies present at the event and Intersect. He engaged in productive dialogues with several companies, presenting Intersect's solutions and discussing potential collaborations.
