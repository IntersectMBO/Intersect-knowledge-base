# Additional Governance features

### Digitalblock Labs LTD

**Grant value: 90,000 ADA**

The grantee will upgrade the Vespr wallet to support standard functionalities within the Conway ledger era. Vesper wallet will be upgraded to the CIP-95 wallet-connector standard, facilitating Conway-related wallet-dApp connections. Users will also have the opportunity to test the Conway-compatible Vespr wallet on a Conway-compatible test network.
