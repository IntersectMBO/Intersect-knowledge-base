# SundaeSwap Labs

**Grant Value : 70,000 ADA**

## Scope:

* Establish a Cardano governance metadata standard

## Deliverables:

1. Respond to comments and as appropriate, update the Cardano governance metadata standard CIP on a weekly basis
2. Respond to comments and provide CIP updates, as appropriate and based on comments, at least once a month
3. Provide a CIP update, with a design based on consolidated  stakeholder and workshop feedback
4. Provide a Cardano governance metadata standard CIP update, with a design based on stakeholder feedback
5. Implement a Rust library for parsing and working with Cardano governance metadata as specified in the CIP
