# Closing Report on the Temporary Membership Review Process

## Executive Summary

Following the publication of the [Statement on the April 2026 Committee Elections](https://docs.intersectmbo.org/intersect-knowledge-base/intersect-membership/intersect-voting-events/2026-intersect-elections/april-2025-committee-elections/april-2025-committee-election-results/statement-about-april-2026-election-results), Intersect initiated the temporary membership review process approved by our governing board. The purpose of the process was to review each affected membership individually, provide members with appropriate opportunities to respond, and enable the Board to make final decisions in accordance with Intersect's Bylaws.

The review process has now been completed and all Board decisions have been implemented.
